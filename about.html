We are a welcoming spiritual community that affirms the sacredness of transgender, gender non-conforming, and intersex lives. We exist to foster belonging, celebrate gender diversity, and support every person's journey toward living authentically.

Our Theological Foundation

We hold that gender diversity is not an accident, a disorder, or a deviation — it is a reflection of the divine. Across cultures and throughout history, those who transcend the boundaries of gender have been recognized as sacred: the Two-Spirit people of Indigenous North America, the hijra of South Asia, the Galli priests of ancient Rome, and the multiple genders named in the Talmud. We stand in this ancient tradition, affirming that trans, gender non-conforming, and intersex individuals carry inherent spiritual meaning. For the full scholarly treatment, explore our Sacred History.
