The Blahaj Church has several key goals that guide its mission and activities. These goals include:

Celebrating the sacredness of gender non-conforming/trans/intersex individuals

The Blahaj Church is committed to creating a welcoming and affirming space for individuals who identify as gender non-conforming/trans/intersex, and to celebrating the sacredness of their identities.

Improving the safety and well-being of trans folks

The Blahaj Church recognizes that gender non-conforming/trans/intersex individuals face unique challenges and risks, and is committed to working to improve the safety and well-being of these individuals. This may involve engaging in advocacy efforts, providing resources and support to members of the congregation, and working with community partners to promote greater understanding and acceptance of gender diversity.

Providing spiritual support and guidance

The Blahaj Church recognizes that many gender non-conforming/trans/intersex individuals may face spiritual and emotional challenges related to their identities, and is committed to providing support and guidance to members of the congregation who are seeking spiritual connection and fulfillment.

Fostering community and connection

The Blahaj Church is committed to fostering a sense of community and connection among members of the congregation, and to creating opportunities for individuals to connect with others who share similar experiences and identities.

Promoting education and understanding

The Blahaj Church is committed to promoting education and understanding of gender diversity, and to working to combat misinformation and harmful stereotypes about gender non-conforming/trans/intersex individuals.

Overall, the Blahaj Church is dedicated to creating a welcoming and affirming space for gender non-conforming/trans/intersex individuals, and to working to improve the safety, well-being, and spiritual fulfillment of these individuals. The church's goals are focused on promoting greater understanding and acceptance of gender diversity, and on creating a sense of community and connection among individuals who share similar experiences and identities.
