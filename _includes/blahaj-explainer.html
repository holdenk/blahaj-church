What is a Blahaj?

"Blahaj" is Swedish for "blue shark" — and also the name of a beloved IKEA stuffed shark that became a symbol of comfort and solidarity in the trans community during the COVID-19 pandemic. We adopted the name as a sign of warmth, safety, and belonging. Learn why Blåhaj became a trans icon. The Blahaj Church is not affiliated with or endorsed by IKEA.
