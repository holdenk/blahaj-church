These goals guide our mission and shape everything we do.

Celebrate the Sacred

We create a welcoming, affirming space for trans, gender non-conforming, and intersex individuals – celebrating the sacredness of their identities through worship, ritual, and community.

Improve Safety

Our community faces unique challenges and risks. We work to improve safety and well-being through advocacy, training, mutual aid, and partnerships with organizations that share our values.

Provide Spiritual Support

Many gender-diverse people face spiritual and emotional challenges related to their identities. We offer support, guidance, and a space for spiritual connection and fulfillment.

Foster Community

We build a sense of belonging and connection among our members, creating opportunities for people to connect with others who share similar experiences and identities.

Promote Understanding

We promote education about gender diversity and work to combat misinformation and harmful stereotypes – both within and beyond our community.
