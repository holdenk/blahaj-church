Recurring Worship Schedule

The Blahaj Church holds regular acts of worship throughout the year. All members and prospective congregants are welcome.

Weekly: Virtual Shot Hangout

Members who inject hormone replacement therapy gather virtually to take their sacred HRT in the company of fellow congregants. A weekly ritual of community, support, and affirmation. Open to all members.

Monthly: Motorcycle Ride to Alice's

A monthly pilgrimage by motorcycle to Alice's Restaurant in the Bay Area. Open to all members – an act of worship celebrating freedom, self-expression, and the open road.

Periodic: HRT Communion

In-person celebrations where members come together to take their HRT as a communal, sacred act. Part party, part ritual – a joyful gathering of affirmation and fellowship.

Quarterly: Gear Day

Members gather to repair and maintain safety gear, share equipment, and discuss safety practices for living as trans, gender non-conforming, or intersex individuals.

Periodic: Bath / Spa Days

Communal bathing and self-care gatherings held in a safe, affirming environment. A ritual of sacred self-care and bodily celebration.

Annual Observances

November 20: Trans Day of Remembrance

A candlelight motorcycle ride and memorial service honoring the lives lost to transphobic violence. The lead minister delivers a sermon and members share reflections in communal remembrance.

March 31: Trans Day of Visibility

A day of celebration and empowerment with sermons, social events, and community gatherings. Members wear trans flag colors and engage in acts of allyship and activism.

June: SF Pride and Trans March

The church participates in San Francisco Pride through the affiliated Sparkling Pink Pandas motorcycle club, with rides, marching, and traffic support for the community.
