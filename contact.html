Where We Meet

The Blahaj Church gathers monthly in the San Francisco Bay Area for worship services, alternating between Oakland (East Bay) and the greater San Francisco area (West Bay). The congregation also meets weekly online for virtual worship.

Because our congregation is small and faces real security concerns due to anti-trans hostility, the specific physical meeting location is shared by email with congregants and those who RSVP in advance of each gathering. If you would like to attend, please contact us at {{ site.email }} and we will share the details for the next service.

Join the Congregation

While no formal registration with the Blahaj Church is required, folks are encouraged to register. One can register by either emailing the church or joining the mailing list. As a community that embraces the divine nature of trans folks, the congregation is open to all who share in our values of love, acceptance, and support.

We welcome anyone who wishes to join us in celebrating the beauty of trans identity and working towards a world where trans individuals are fully accepted and valued. By joining, one can also participate in the church's events, discussions, and initiatives aimed at promoting the well-being of trans people everywhere.

Training Reimbursement

The Blahaj Church offers training reimbursement to board members, officers, and congregants on a case-by-case basis. All are strongly encouraged to apply. The board, treasurer, or first minister, will consider requests for reimbursement based on relevance to the church's mission, quality and cost of the training.

Some sample training that the board would be likely to approve include:

  • Safety training – first aid, CPR, de-escalation techniques, personal safety, or vehicle safety courses
  • Self-defense – martial arts, self-defense classes, or personal safety workshops
  • Public speaking – effective presentations and community engagement

If you are interested in applying for training reimbursement, please contact the church office.

Grants for Affirming Documents

Having legal documents reflect a person's lived gender can be important for reducing harassment and allowing access to essential services. To that end, the church encourages members seeking affirming documents for whom cost is an issue to apply for grants to cover the cost.
