Ecclesiastical Government

The ecclesiastical government of the Blahaj Church is structured to ensure that decisions are made democratically and in the best interest of the community. Currently, there is only one Blahaj Church. The Church is led by the First Minister, who is responsible for setting the Church's agenda, managing its resources, and ensuring that the Church's mission and values are upheld.

First Minister

The First Minister is advised by the board, which serve as the Church's governing body. The board members are responsible for making major decisions, setting policies, and ensuring that the Church remains true to its mission and values.

Regional Churches

If and when additional regional churches are created, they will operate autonomously but will be expected to adhere to the same principles and values as the Blahaj Church. Each regional church will have its own First Minister and board, and decisions will be made through democratic processes that involve all members of the regional church. Should a regional church wish to split from the original Blahaj Church of San Francisco on religious or other grounds, it will be expected to change its name. There will be no fee from the central Blahaj Church to others, but they may wish to collaborate financially on important matters.

Overall, the ecclesiastical government of the Blahaj Church is designed to ensure that all members have a voice in the Church's decisions and that the Church remains true to its mission of promoting love, acceptance, and inclusivity.

Doctrine and Discipline

As a religious institution focused on the sacredness of transgender, gender non-conforming, and intersex individuals, the Blahaj Church has established the following formal code of doctrine and discipline:

Doctrine

Sacredness of Gender Diversity
The Blahaj Church recognizes and affirms the inherent value and sacredness of all gender identities and expressions. We believe that every individual is created in the image of the divine and deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.
Inclusivity
The Blahaj Church welcomes and affirms all individuals, regardless of their gender identity or expression. We strive to create a safe and welcoming space for all people.
Hormone Replacement Therapy
The Blahaj Church recognizes the importance of HRT for individuals who desire it as a crucial tool in helping individuals live and present in their congruent gender(s).
Puberty Blockers
The Blahaj Church recognizes the value of puberty blockers for young people experiencing gender dysphoria to help alleviate the distress caused by incongruence.
Gender-Affirming Rituals
The Blahaj Church holds gender-affirming rituals that celebrate and honor the sacredness of transgender, gender non-conforming, and intersex individuals.
Self Determination of One's Bodies
The Blahaj Church believes strongly in the act of self determination of medical and cultural issues of one's body.

Discipline

Respect for Diversity
The Blahaj Church requires that all members and leaders treat others with respect and dignity, regardless of their gender identity or expression. Discrimination or harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.
Accountability
The Blahaj Church holds all members and leaders accountable for their actions and words. Any behavior that is harmful, discriminatory, or disrespectful will be addressed and corrected.
Confidentiality
The Blahaj Church respects the privacy and confidentiality of its members. Personal information shared within the community will be kept confidential unless the individual gives explicit permission to share.
Responsibility
The Blahaj Church expects all members and leaders to take responsibility for their own actions and words.
Community Service
The Blahaj Church encourages its members and leaders to engage in community service and activism that supports the rights and well-being of transgender, gender non-conforming, and intersex individuals.

The Blahaj Church recognizes that this code of doctrine and discipline is not exhaustive and may need to be amended or updated over time. However, we believe that it reflects our core values and principles as a religious institution focused on the sacredness of gender diversity.

Statement of Inter-Faith Support

The Blahaj Church recognizes that there are many religious beliefs and practices around the world that may not align with our specific focus on the sacredness of transgender, gender non-conforming, and intersex individuals. However, we believe that it is possible to maintain compatibility with other religious beliefs and practices that do not conflict with our mission.

The majority of members of the Blahaj Church are not members of other congregations.

As a community, we are committed to engaging in interfaith dialogue and building relationships with individuals and organizations that share our values of inclusivity and respect for diversity. We believe that by seeking common ground and working towards shared goals, we can create a more just and compassionate world for all.

Furthermore, we acknowledge that individuals may have multiple religious affiliations or may identify with multiple spiritual traditions. The Blahaj Church respects and honors the diversity of individual beliefs and practices, and we encourage individuals to integrate their spiritual journey with their gender affirmation journey in a way that feels authentic and meaningful to them.

Board

The board of the Blahaj Church is composed of five people the majority of whom are transgender, gender non-conforming, gender variant or intersex individuals. The board serves as the governing body of the church, overseeing its operations and making decisions on matters such as budgeting, fundraising, and outreach. The board members serve one-year terms.

The lead minister, who may also use the title of priest or priestess, both reports to the board and works closely with it to ensure that the church's mission and values are upheld. The lead minister may serve on the board.

The structure of the board reflects the Blahaj Church's commitment to inclusivity and empowerment of marginalized communities. By placing decision-making power in the hands of those who have experienced discrimination and oppression based on their gender identity or expression, the Church seeks to create a community that is truly representative and reflective of the diverse needs and perspectives of its members.

In addition to its governing responsibilities, the board is also tasked with investigating any accusations of wrongdoing by ministers or members of the congregation. If it is found that an individual has engaged in wrongdoing, the board has the authority to take disciplinary action, which may include suspension or expulsion from the Church.

Political Activity

The Blahaj Church does not participate in or intervene in any political campaign on behalf of or in opposition to any candidate for public office. No officer, minister, or representative of the church shall endorse candidates, make political contributions on behalf of the church, or issue statements of position for or against any candidate while acting in their official church capacity.

This prohibition is consistent with the requirements of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. The church may engage in issue-based advocacy and education consistent with its religious mission, provided that such activities do not constitute a substantial part of its overall activities.

Appointed Positions

The Blahaj Church's board appoints standard officer roles to assist with the management of the church:

Treasurer
Responsible for managing the church's finances, keeping accurate records of all financial transactions, and providing regular financial reports to the board and congregation.
Secretary
Responsible for taking minutes at board meetings, maintaining records of church policies and procedures, and ensuring that all legal and regulatory requirements are met.
Membership Chair
Responsible for managing the church's membership database, processing new membership applications, and communicating with members about church events and activities.
Communications Chair
Responsible for managing the church's website, social media accounts, and other communication channels, and ensuring that all communications are consistent with the church's mission and values.
Outreach Chair
Responsible for promoting the church's mission and values to the broader community, organizing community events and service projects, and building relationships with other organizations.
