Where We Meet

The Blahaj Church gathers monthly in the San Francisco Bay Area, alternating between Oakland (East Bay) and the greater San Francisco area (West Bay) so that congregants from across the region can participate. The specific meeting location is shared with congregants by email in advance of each gathering. Because the congregation is small and faces real security concerns due to anti-trans hostility, we do not publish the exact address publicly.

To receive meeting details, join our mailing list.

Recurring Worship Schedule

The Blahaj Church holds regular acts of worship throughout the year. All members and prospective congregants are welcome.

Monthly: Worship Gathering

The congregation's primary worship service, held monthly in the Bay Area. Includes an opening reflection, community check-in, sermon, optional HRT Communion, prayers, and closing blessing. See Acts of Worship for the full order of service.

Weekly: Virtual Shot Hangout

Members who inject hormone replacement therapy gather virtually to take their sacred HRT in the company of fellow congregants. A weekly ritual of community, support, and affirmation. Open to all members.

Periodic: HRT Communion

In-person celebrations where members come together to take their HRT as a communal, sacred act. A joyful gathering of affirmation and fellowship.

Quarterly: Gear Day

Members gather to repair and maintain safety gear, share equipment, and discuss safety practices for living as trans, gender non-conforming, or intersex individuals.

Periodic: Bath / Spa Days

Communal bathing and self-care gatherings held in a safe, affirming environment. A ritual of sacred self-care and bodily celebration.

Monthly: Motorcycle Pilgrimage to Alice's

A monthly group ride to Alice's Restaurant in the Bay Area, organized through the affiliated Sparkling Pink Pandas. An act of worship celebrating freedom and self-expression. Non-riders welcome to meet at the destination.

Annual Observances

November 20: Trans Day of Remembrance

A candlelight vigil and memorial service honoring the lives lost to transphobic violence. The First Minister delivers a sermon and members share reflections in communal remembrance.

March 31: Trans Day of Visibility

A day of celebration and empowerment with sermons, social events, and community gatherings. Members wear trans flag colors and engage in acts of allyship and activism.

June: SF Pride and Trans March

The church participates in San Francisco Pride through the affiliated Sparkling Pink Pandas, with marching and community support.
