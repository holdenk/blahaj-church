Whether you've been part of the trans community for years or are just beginning to explore, there's a place for you here. Here's how to connect with us.

Ways to Participate

Join the Mailing List

Email {{ site.email }} to receive gathering details and community updates. It's the easiest way to stay connected.

Attend a Gathering

We meet monthly in the San Francisco Bay Area and bi-weekly online. New members and those exploring are welcome without commitment. See our gatherings.

Become a Congregant

No formal registration is required, but we appreciate it. Simply email us or join the mailing list, and you are part of the community.

Volunteer

Help organize events, maintain the website, or support community initiatives. Every contribution strengthens what we're building together.

Start a Chapter

Interested in starting a Blahaj Church community in your area? We'd love to help you get started. Contact us to learn more.

Become a Minister

Learn about our ministerial training program and how you can serve the community. Explore ministerial training.

Tax status: The Blahaj Church is incorporated in California, our IRS 501(c)(3) recognition is pending, and donations are not currently tax-deductible.

Training Reimbursement

We Invest in Your Growth

The Blahaj Church believes that building skills is a sacred act of self-care and community care. We offer training reimbursement to board members, officers, and congregants on a case-by-case basis. Everyone is strongly encouraged to apply.

Training we are especially eager to support includes:

  • Safety training: first aid, CPR, de-escalation techniques, personal safety, or vehicle safety courses
  • Self-defense: including self-defense classes, or personal safety workshops
  • Public speaking: effective presentations and community engagement skills

If you're interested, reach out to the Church office at {{ site.email }}. The board, treasurer, or First Minister will consider requests based on their relevance to our mission, as well as the quality and cost of the training, how many can participate, and our budget.

Grants for Affirming Documents

Your Documents Should Reflect Who You Are

Having legal documents that reflect your lived gender can make a real difference from reducing harassment to opening access to essential services. We know that cost can be a barrier, and we don't want it to stand in your way.

The Blahaj Church offers grants to help cover the cost of obtaining affirming legal documents. If this is something you need, we encourage you to apply by contacting us at {{ site.email }} (you need not be a member of the church).

Board Opportunities

Our board frequently has open seats, and we aim to welcome new voices. Board members can serve terms of varying length and help guide the direction of the church. As part of our commitment to the community we serve, a majority of board seats must be held by trans, gender non-conforming, or intersex individuals.

If you're interested in helping shape the future of the Blahaj Church, we'd love to hear from you. Learn more about our governance structure, or email us at {{ site.email }} to express your interest.
