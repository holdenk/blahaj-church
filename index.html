Blahaj Church

A welcoming and inclusive religious community affirming the sacredness of trans, gender non-conforming, and intersex individuals.

Learn About Us

Our Mission

The mission of the Blahaj Church is to create a welcoming and inclusive community that supports the spiritual growth and well-being of all individuals who identify as transgender, gender non-conforming, and/or non-binary. We seek to foster a sense of belonging and acceptance, where members can freely explore and express their gender identity without fear of discrimination or prejudice.

We are committed to promoting social justice, advocating for the rights and dignity of transgender individuals, and creating positive change in the wider community. Our ultimate goal is to create a world where all individuals, regardless of gender identity, are treated with respect, dignity, and equality.

Our Core Beliefs

Sacredness of Gender Diversity

We recognize and honor the diversity of gender identities and expressions as sacred and valuable. All trans*, intersex, and gender non-conforming individuals are created in the image of the divine.

Love is Love

We recognize and celebrate all consenting adult relationships, regardless of gender. Everyone has the right to love and be loved without discrimination.

Community & Safety

Because this community has historically been marginalized, we believe that taking time to improve one's safety is a sacred act of self love. We foster connection and mutual support.
