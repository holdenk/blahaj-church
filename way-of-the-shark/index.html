Welcome to this collection of stories, teachings, and testimonies — a testament to the diverse experiences of those who walk the Way of the Shark. Here you will find accounts of struggle, triumph, heartbreak, and joy. Some are allegorical, told through the figure of Blahaj as a guide and companion. Others are drawn directly from the lived experiences of our congregation. All are sacred.

These are not mere anecdotes. They are the living scripture of a community that has learned, often at great cost, that softness is strength, that chosen family is real family, and that the smallest acts of defiance can carry the weight of the divine.
