Introduction

The ministerial training program for prospective ministers in the Blahaj Church is focused on providing a comprehensive understanding of the lived experiences of gender non-conforming/trans/intersex individuals and preparing individuals to be effective leaders within the community.

Training

One of the core components of the training program is the reading of "Trans Bodies, Trans Selves" (or similar more updated work by and for trans people), which is a comprehensive guide to the experiences, needs, and resources for individuals who are gender non-conforming/trans/intersex. This text provides valuable insights and perspectives on the challenges and experiences of these individuals, and is an essential resource for anyone wishing to serve as a minister within the Blahaj Church.

In addition to this foundational reading, prospective ministers are required to go on a journey of self-reflection to explore their own reasons for wanting to become a minister and to ensure that they are committed to upholding the values and mission of the church. This process of self-reflection may involve journaling, discussions with other members of the church, and guidance from the lead minister and board.

Prospective ministers are also trained in some common safety items, such as how injections are done and how to administer naloxone. This training is designed to ensure that ministers are able to provide support and assistance to members of the congregation who may be in need of medical assistance or other forms of care. Ministers are not intended to replace medical staff, but to provide support in situations where traditional care is not immediately available – for example, responding to an overdose at a community gathering or during a group outing.

Prospective ministers will practice their public speaking – both in the context of speaking to members of the congregation at services but also for when faced with complicated questions from outside of the church. This can include how to make statements of support for our trans colleagues.

Prospective ministers will also be asked to take some time to reflect on if they want to participate in evangelism, or focus on supporting existing members.

Overall, the ministerial training program in the Blahaj Church is designed to provide individuals with a comprehensive understanding of the experiences of gender non-conforming/trans/intersex individuals and to prepare them to be effective leaders within the community.

Expulsion

The Blahaj Church is committed to maintaining professional boundaries and preventing potential impropriety in all aspects of our ministry, including ministerial training. As such, it is strictly prohibited for ministers to engage in any romantic or sexual relationships with prospective ministers who are currently in the training program. This policy is in place to ensure that all individuals participating in the program can do so without fear of coercion or favoritism, and that the integrity of the training process is maintained. The board may grant an exception for pre-existing relationships but then the minister involved should not participate in the candidate's training. Any violation of this policy will result in immediate disciplinary action, up to and including expulsion from the ministry.

Become a Minister

  • Complete the training
  • Be willing to submit to a background check (with the understanding that crimes of survival will not be counted negatively)
  • (Optional) perform an internship with another minister
  • Be voted in by consensus of the board

If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.

A minister does not have any powers besides preaching on behalf of the church unless the board, or first/high minister, delegates such powers to the minister.
