How We Gather

Community is at the heart of everything we do. The Blahaj Church gathers in a rhythm that keeps us connected, cared for, and grounded in our shared faith: monthly in-person worship in the San Francisco Bay Area, weekly virtual gatherings, quarterly gear days, and annual observances marking the sacred moments of our calendar.

Whether you join us on a motorcycle ride, over a video call, or at our monthly service, you are part of this community. We rotate between meeting at different Bay Area locations aiming to alternate between Oakland (East Bay) and the greater San Francisco area (West Bay), so that congregants across the region can participate.

Sparkling Pink Pandas

Our Affiliation with SPP

The Blahaj Church maintains a close affiliation with the Sparkling Pink Pandas (SPP), a trans-focused motorcycle and scooter group based in San Francisco. SPP is not a ministry of the church – it is an independent community organization with its own history and mission – but we share members, values, and a deep commitment to the safety and visibility of trans people.

Our bi-monthly motorcycle pilgrimages are organized through SPP, and we ride together at SF Pride and the Trans March each June. The relationship between the church and the Pandas is one of mutual respect and shared purpose: two communities, side by side, looking out for each other.

You do not need to be a member of the Sparkling Pink Pandas to join the Church or vice-versa.

We would love to have you join us.
