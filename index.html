Blahaj Church

A spiritual community honoring the sacredness of gender diversity. We gather in affirmation, solidarity, and care for trans, nonbinary, and gender-expansive people everywhere.

We are an active spiritual community rooted in the Bay Area — not satire, not symbolic, but a real gathering of people building something sacred.

Community Life

We are not just a set of beliefs. We are people who gather, celebrate, and care for one another.

  • Monthly Worship

    In-person gatherings in the Bay Area with reflection, community check-in, sermon, and blessing.

  • Weekly Virtual Hangouts

    Online gatherings for connection and support, wherever you are.

  • Quarterly Gear Days

    Safety gear repair, knowledge sharing, and building mutual protection together.

  • Motorcycle Pilgrimages

    Monthly rides celebrating freedom and self-expression on the open road.
